# Credits

`cat.gif` is assembled from the creature sprite sheet in the
[1-Bit Platformer Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/1-bit-platformer-pack-0)
by Zanin, published on OpenGameArt under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The four
16 by 16 frames are scaled eight times with nearest-neighbor sampling and
played in a loop. `cat-icon.gif` is the same animation cropped to the
cat and scaled three times, for the comment heading.
